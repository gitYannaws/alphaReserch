# Sales Safari finds gripes, not buyers

**Verdict: no.** On its two production runs the Sales Safari pipeline does not find software-shaped, paid demand in community text; it ranks long-lived forum complaints and arguments. Three measured facts settle it. First, the precision gate (stage 3b) has been switched off since 2026-08-17, so **72,320 of 74,852 stored pains on run `pbfamily0001` (96.6%) were never judged**, and in the 290-pain sample that *was* judged inside ranked themes only **24 (8.3%) are product-shaped** (`product_friction` 17, `cost_complaint` 7, `wish` 0); the project's own gold set puts the extractor at **31.8% precision alone, 60% with the gate on**. Second, the ranked output is not software demand: of the **top 25 themes on `pbfamily0001`, nine are complaints about Tinder, seven are forum-argument chatter** ("frustration with people" at rank 12, "wasting time and resources" at rank 25, both judged software-solvable `yes` at 0.8–1.0 by the local 14B model), and three are two-sided-marketplace wishes; on the second run `2a926f2fad56`, **zero of 14 ranked themes are software-shaped** and the only `yes` is "subreddit needs more moderation". Third, "paid" is the weakest signal in the formula: willingness-to-pay sits at its floor of 1.0 on **526 of 787 ranked themes (67%)** and has the lowest rank correlation of any component (**Spearman +0.23**), while persistence, which rewards a complaint for being old, is the strongest (**+0.62**). Beneath all three is a choice the method itself warns against: every run mined consumer lifestyle subreddits (passport bros, expats, digital nomads), the audience Hoy and Hillman say to "run away" from, so no scoring fix alone can make the output product-shaped. The engineering is sound where it counts (verbatim-quote gate, distinct-author counting, resumability, auditable score breakdowns); the signal design is not. Most fixes are cheap and are listed in priority order below; the two with the most leverage are turning the precision gate back on for ranked themes and reframing the software gate from "could software solve it" to "would someone buy software for it".

Evidence conventions: file paths are relative to `/home/monny/Projects/alphaResearch/sales-safari/` at commit `a1f2acb`; "DB" means a read-only SQL query over `db/safari.sqlite` (snapshot 2026-08-23, queried 2026-11-10). External claims carry inline links.

## Every run mined an audience the method tells you to avoid

Sales Safari filters at the audience level before it reads a single post. Amy Hoy's rule is "Pick an audience that buys things. One you can study, reach, and persuade," and her example of failure is salon software: "Those salons didn't want to pay for software" ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/pizza-crust-problems/)). The 30x500 eligibility page lists the audiences that do not work: local small businesses, artists, students outside demanding majors, and "audiences known for complaining, laziness, poor business skills or being broke: Run away, as fast as your little legs can carry you," alongside audiences that do "driveby bitching" ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/30x500/is-30x500-right-for-me/)). Hillman prefers B2B outright because "consumer audiences are fickle and really inconsistent" ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/podcast/ep42-what-is-sales-safari-with-eteinne-garbugli/)). Jason Cohen's bootstrapper rules say the same from a different direction: sell "only to businesses (B2B), never consumers," avoid marketplaces "which require attracting two sides at once," and prefer "naturally recurring pain, such as hosting, invoicing, compliance" ([Source](https://mtlynch.io/notes/designing-the-ideal-bootstrapped-business/)). YC's tarpit list is almost entirely consumer discovery and coordination ideas, "a problem that almost everyone encounters at some point. And it seems like it would be so easy to solve" ([Source](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-evaluate-startup-ideas-startup-school/index.md)).

The pipeline has no stage that scores the community, and the corpora it was pointed at fail the test by construction. The DB holds **13 passport-bros subreddits merged into run `pbfamily0001` (186,925 documents)**, a single-subreddit run `2a926f2fad56` (3,379 documents), unscored corpora for r/expats, r/digitalnomad and three Thailand/Philippines expat subs, and two forums (glp1forum.com, geomax.me) that produced zero pains and zero clusters. The consequence shows in what reached the top. On `2a926f2fad56` the 14 ranked themes are "concern about safety when traveling solo," "specific shared customer problem" (a placeholder label echoed back by the model), "subreddit needs more moderation," "struggling with virginity and dating in a hookup culture," "being robbed." On `pbfamily0001`, **13 of the 21 drafted ideas are dating-app products or Tinder add-ons** (Verify, SwipeCheck, ProfileCheck, MatchGuard, Arrival, Compass, ShowUp, TandemDate, Intent, EvenQueue, OneMatch, WhereToSwipe, StatusShare), and **four of the 21 are the same product** (check a dating profile's authenticity). A dating app is Cohen's bad market (two-sided marketplace) and YC's canonical consumer tarpit at the same time.

The automated-tool literature reports the same thing from the other side. BigIdeasDB's benchmark of ten LLM extractors on a 900-record human gold standard found "Every model performed worst on Reddit — average F1 0.599 vs 0.808 on Capterra," because "Reddit users rarely state problems directly" and threads carry "off-topic replies, meta-discussion, sarcasm, and community humor that sounds like a complaint but is not" ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/llm-benchmark-pain-point-extraction)). The MonetScope builder found niche professional subreddits (r/MedicalCoding, r/SmallFarms) "have 10x less noise and 10x more specific workflow language" than general ones ([Source](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-spent-a-month-building-a-tool-that-reads-reddit-hn-forstartup-ideas-heres-what-real-pain-actually-looks-like-d961673b21)). Audience choice is a config decision upstream of every line of code below; the rest of this report assumes it is fixed in parallel, because without that the ranking can only pick the least bad gripe.

## Eleven of twelve ranked pains are noise, and clustering shreds the rest

Stage 3a is deliberately recall-first. The prompt at `pipeline/extract.py:22-27` says "Cast a WIDE net - your job is RECALL. A separate pass removes false positives later," and includes "a gripe about people / places / dating / life" in its definition of pain; `extract.py:35-36` adds "Do NOT self-censor advice, opinion, or debate." That design is defensible only if the separate pass runs. It does not: `config.yaml:293` sets `verify.enabled: false` and `:296` sets `require_verdict: false`, `store.get_pains` keeps `verified IS NULL` (`store.py:1263-1272`), and `run_progress` shows stage 3 at `0/0` on both production runs. The 2,508 `verified=0` rows on `pbfamily0001` are almost all (2,242) a deterministic prune of spans under 25 characters by `scripts/prune_short_spans.py`, not an LLM judgement. Only about 290 pains carry an LLM `pain_type`, and they came from a sample spread across *ranked* themes: `advice_opinion` 138, `off_topic` 66, `social_complaint` 62, `product_friction` 17, `cost_complaint` 7. The single-pass decision document predicted "roughly three noise candidates for every four extracted"; the measured slice is eleven in twelve. `CLAUDE.md:142` states the rule the project then broke: "Turning 3b off without re-tightening 3a leaves the pipeline with no precision gate at all."

The benchmark numbers that justified the choice do not describe production. The synthetic hybrid run `cb39389524e2` is cited in `config.yaml:148-153` at 81.2% recall / 98.5% precision, but `build_synth_corpus.py:109` lists only synthetic distractors in `noise_source_urls` and `score_synth.py:50, 70-76` builds the false-positive denominator from that list, so a real post flagged as a pain is neither a hit nor a miss. On that very run the DB shows 903 candidates of which verify kept 92 (10.2%). The human gold set (`db/gold-results.json`, 112 docs, 10 pain docs, all under 500 characters) is the number that describes production: qwen2.5 at 70% recall / 31.8% precision alone, 30% / 60% with verify; Sonnet at 100% / 25.6%. Every confidence interval spans 30 or more points because there are ten positive documents, the 200-document round-2 sample with long-document strata has never been labelled, and `score_gold.py:470-480` keys its cache on labels and seed but not `PROMPT_VERSION`, which the 2026-07-18 benchmark note itself flagged as mixing prompt versions in one table. The literature expects exactly this deflation: Dąbrowski et al.'s replication found the SAFE feature extractor's reported 0.70 precision / 0.56 recall fell to 0.32 / 0.29 on an independent, larger annotated set, because the original evaluation was "limited to a fraction of app reviews from which the tool could first correctly extract features" ([Source](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10164064/1/final-version.pdf)). BigIdeasDB's recommendation from its own benchmark is the architecture this pipeline has and does not run: "A two-stage extract-then-verify pipeline — a high-recall model to cast a wide net, a high-precision model to confirm — can beat any single model" ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/llm-benchmark-pain-point-extraction)).

Three smaller leaks add noise before clustering. Reddit and Arctic Shift text is stored with quoted lines intact (`reddit_collector.py:103-104`, `arcticshift_collector.py:150-168`), so a span lifted from a quote is attributed to the replier; the DB has **309 identical `verbatim_span` values appearing in more than one pain**, which inflates both size and `distinct_authors`. There is no bot filter anywhere in the collectors or `extract.py`, so AutoModerator rule text is one `author_hash` with thousands of documents feeding 3a. Arctic Shift comments are emitted with `title=""` (`arcticshift_collector.py:164-166`), so for every comment in the main corpus the extractor and the judge see no thread context, while the live Reddit collector does pass it (`reddit_collector.py:317, 329-331`).

Clustering then discards half of what survives and fragments the remainder. `config.yaml:317-318` sets `min_cluster_size: 2` and `cluster_selection_method: leaf`; `min_samples` is not set anywhere, so hdbscan defaults it to 2. On `pbfamily0001`, **39,182 of 72,344 eligible pains (54.2%) are HDBSCAN noise** and get no row anywhere: `cluster.py:288` returns the count in a dict and `resume.py:375` logs only `clusters=`, so nobody can audit whether those 39,182 were junk or signal. The 33,162 that clustered became **8,816 themes with a mean of 3.76 members and a maximum of 23**; 6,648 themes (75%) have fewer than five members and only five have 20 or more. A 180,000-document corpus produced no theme larger than 23 pains. The effect on the one unit the project got right, distinct authors, is that it is measured on fragments: **23 ranked labels mention fake profiles, scams or bots, 171 authors combined, none individually above 13**. Published BERTopic runs report comparable outlier shares (59% on 24,752 Dutch narratives, 35.3% on 364,000 Reddit sentences) but at `min_cluster_size` 5–15 and 50 respectively ([Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14707); [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17658)); the RE-literature setting is 30 ([Source](https://kblincoe.github.io/publications/2022_RE_Clusters.pdf)). The HDBSCAN docs say the outlier share "is handled by the `min_samples` parameter," which "is automatically set to the same value of `min_cluster_size`" unless fixed lower, and that `cluster_selection_epsilon` exists so dense regions are "not split up any further" ([Source](https://maartengr.github.io/BERTopic/faq.html); [Source](https://hdbscan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/parameter_selection.html)). None of the three knobs the docs name is used. No theme-quality number (cohesion, DBCV, membership probability) is persisted on `clusters` (`store.py:172-178`), and `_merge_duplicate_labels` merges only byte-identical labels (`cluster.py:193-207`).

## The ranking rewards age and generic words, and the software gate says yes to insults

Stage 6 computes five components and stage 9 multiplies. `demand_score = (3·intensity + 3·frequency + 3·wtp + 2·reach + 3·recurrence) / 14` (`s6_demand.py:149-151, :310`; weights at `config.yaml:357-362`), and `rank_score = demand_score × persistence_score × solvable_weight` (`s9_rank.py:113`). Nothing in stage 6 is LLM-judged: intensity, willingness-to-pay and reachability are regex term lists, frequency and recurrence are counts, persistence is a date span. Term matching is "distinct terms hit anywhere in the concatenated text of every member pain" (`_term_hits`, `:102-109`), so one member's "premium" lights the whole theme. Measured over the 787 ranked themes:

| Component | Formula | sd | At its floor | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| intensity | `3.0 + 1.5 × distinct hits` | 1.56 | 321/787 (41%) at 3.0 | hard 94, frustrated 92, can't 78, difficult 56, problem 52; **141 themes have only generic hits** |
| frequency | log of `authors + 0.5·extra posts + upvotes` | 0.70 | — | rho with size +0.94; upvotes add at most 3 of 25 voices |
| willingness to pay | `1.0 + 2.5·strong + 0.6·weak` | 1.43 | **526/787 (67%) at 1.0** | money 143, pay 56, expensive 48; only 73 (9%) hit a strong term; "scam" is a strong term |
| reachability | `2.0 + 1.5·hits` + post-level share | 1.49 | 497/787 (63%) at 4.0 | regex for "forum", "sub", "app", "tinder" |
| recurrence | threads + workarounds + repeated 8-grams | 0.75 | — | rho with size +0.80 |
| persistence | `1 + 6·log(span months) + 3·recent share` | 1.15 | — | multiplier, not averaged; rho with size +0.07 |

Spearman correlation with final rank, n=787: **persistence +0.62, demand +0.60, recurrence +0.38, intensity +0.37, size +0.34, authors +0.33, frequency +0.33, reach +0.31, WTP +0.23**. Themes with WTP ≥ 4 (n=72) have a median rank of 156; themes at the WTP floor (n=526) a median rank of 450. So the order is first "how many months does this span and is it recent," then "how many people and how many pain words," and last "does anyone mention paying." Two structural consequences follow. The persistence curve gives roughly 9 points to a theme first raised in 2022 and still raised in 2026, and tops out near 4–5 for a pain that appeared last year regardless of volume, so **an emerging pain cannot reach the top**. And because `voices` is "full" at 25 while the ranked population has 4–23 authors, frequency spans only 5.49–9.98; the maximum demand gain a 23-author theme can take over a 4-author one is about 0.96 points, less than two generic intensity words. The three largest ranked themes (23, 22, 20 authors) sit at ranks 115, 119 and 350. `CLAUDE.md:145`'s principle "One loud user is not a market" survives as an entry gate, not a ranking force.

The willingness-to-pay design contradicts the strongest practitioner rule on the subject. The Mom Test's rule is "Anything involving the future is an over-optimistic lie," and the question that separates "I-will-pay-to-solve-that problems" from "thats-kind-of-annoying-but-I-can-deal-with-it" is "What are the implications of that?", answered by "How much does the problem cost them? How much do they currently pay to solve it?" ([Source](https://theforge.mcmaster.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/The-Mom-Test.pdf)). Arvid Kahl's version is that someone "looking for alternatives" to a paid tool carries a budget and a price anchor, and that "Whenever you see someone trying to build an Excel sheet or a Google Doc to enable them to solve an issue, you have found an issue exhibiting several traits of a critical problem" ([Source](https://thebootstrappedfounder.com/the-shape-of-a-problem-in-the-wild/)). The pipeline has no tense, no currency amount, no "I pay for X" pattern (`dollars` is a weak term worth 0.6), and the workaround field, which Hoy calls "all the things they're doing around it, that aren't solving it" ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/podcast/ep12-im-shipping-ebombs-now-what-from-pain-to-product-with-nick-piegari/)) and BigIdeasDB calls "the sound of a gap" ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/docs/pain-point-analysis)), is filled on **2% of pains (1,759 of 74,852)**, excluded from the embedding text (`embed.py:18`), and worth at most 0.43 demand points inside recurrence (`s6_demand.py:202`). Stage 7c (`s7c_sentiment.py`), which computes a "seeking" versus "resigned" stance and an urgency score, says in its own header it is "NOT wired into the orchestrator" and has **0 rows** for `pbfamily0001`.

The software preference enters in exactly one place, stage 7b, and it is the wrong question asked of the wrong model on too little text. `s7b_softfilter.py:24-43` asks "could software solve it"; the judge is `qwen2.5:14b-instruct` (`config.yaml:164`) seeing the first six pains of a theme at 240 characters each with no verbatim span (`:46-52`). Its verdicts on `pbfamily0001`: "frustration with people" → `yes 0.80 "Software can manage emotions and responses."`; "frustration with baseless accusations and derailment of topics" → `yes 0.80 "Software can track and manage discussions."`; "wasting time and resources" → `yes 1.00`; "slow response times in communication" (dating texting: "Look at you double texting. No wonder you're single") → `yes 0.90`. Because `rank.allowed_solvability: ["yes", "partial"]` (`config.yaml:390`) excludes `no` and `unknown` entirely, the verdict is a hard gate, the `0.25` and `0.5` entries in `solvable_weights` are dead config, and the only continuous software preference is `yes` 1.0 versus `partial` 0.6. 45 of the top 50 are `yes`. The soft-filter warnings that would catch the actual failure shapes barely fire: `closed_api_dependency` **2 times in the whole run** while Tinder-dependent themes fill ranks 1–4; `two_sided_marketplace` 6 times while "a dating app targeting passport bros" sits at rank 7. `platform_meta` is the only warning in `rank.drop_warnings`, and it requires a member share of 0.5, so "spamming issues" (share 0.29, rank 6), "derailment of topics" (0.00, rank 11) and "writing is difficult to read" (0.17, rank 17) all escaped. `soft_filters.confidence` is stored (`store.py:1697`) and never read by rank.

What the gate should ask is well documented. Migicovsky's three interview facts are "how much does this problem cost them today ... how frequently do they encounter this problem ... how large is their budget," with the assembly-line operator who has the problem hourly but "just don't have the budget, they don't have the authority" as the canonical false positive ([Source](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-1-recap-kevin-hale-and-eric-migicovsky)). Cohen's model multiplies Plausible, Self-Aware, Lucrative, Liquid, Eager and Enduring "Because this is a series of 'ands'" ([Source](https://longform.asmartbear.com/problem/)). Dunford's first competitor is the status quo: "a spreadsheet, a manual process, or hiring an intern" ([Source](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/positioning-and-competition)). Friedman's tarpit test is "First, Google it" ([Source](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-evaluate-startup-ideas-startup-school/index.md)), and the Traction Lab buyer map asks whether the complainer is a user, beneficiary, payer, approver or signer, warning that "If your primary contact fills fewer than three of these roles, you're probably talking to the wrong person" ([Source](https://tractionthinking.substack.com/p/dont-sell-to-users-who-cant-buy)). The LLM-judge literature adds how to ask: binary or pairwise verdicts rather than scales, since absolute scoring is "less robust than relative comparing" ([Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.15594)); criteria decomposed into observable evidence fields; and validation against roughly 100 expert-labelled examples per failure mode with true-positive and true-negative rates reported separately ([Source](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/)). MonetScope's builder learned the same lesson the hard way: "AI is surprisingly bad at distinguishing 'rant' from 'need' out of the box ... I had to build explicit scoring for pain signal, demand signal, and willingness-to-pay before outputs got useful" ([Source](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-spent-a-month-building-a-tool-that-reads-reddit-hn-forstartup-ideas-heres-what-real-pain-actually-looks-like-d961673b21)).

## The idea chain drafts from world knowledge and quotes reviews of the wrong app

Stage order undermines the research stages. `resume.py:496-502` runs ideas (10a) before competitors (9b), and `competitors_run` begins with `store.clear_competitors` (`s9b_competitors.py:162`), so the `competitors:[names], review_gaps:[...]` fields the 10a prompt declares (`s10_ideas.py:18-20, :57-75`) are **always empty on a fresh run**; the "WEDGE versus the named competitors" it asks for is drafted from world knowledge. The current config sets `model: ""` for ideas, competitors and brief (`config.yaml:437, 448, 476`), and `research_cfg` returns the config unchanged when `model` is falsy (`extract.py:300-316`), dropping the research system prompt too, so the next run executes 9b/10a/10b on `qwen2.5:14b-instruct`, the model `CLAUDE.md:41-43` records as having "found competitors for 8 of 642 themes and named mostly magazines." The 21 ideas in the DB were made by a strong model before that change and are not reproducible with the current config. Even with the strong model, the drafting step skipped **26 of the top 50 ranked themes** as not product-shaped (`db/resume-pbfamily0001.log`: `ideas=24 (llm=24 template=0 skipped=26)`), i.e. the drafting model disagreed with the 7b gate on half its input, after which `s10_ideas.py:283-286` takes the top 50 as-is with no dedupe, producing four profile-verification ideas.

The competitor step is the part that works: 40 competitors across 18 themes, all real products with live URLs (Social Catfish, TinEye, Garbo, Google Flights, Skyscanner, Hopper, Bumble, iVisa, Splitwise). Keeping saturation out of the rank formula (pinned by `tests/test_idea_chain.py:176-185`) is also right by the literature: "A crowded market is actually a good sign, because it means both that there's demand and that none of the existing solutions are good enough" ([Source](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html)). The review step is not. Reviews come only from the iTunes Search API, countries `[ph, us]`, `mostrecent`, rating ≤ 2 (`reviews.py:18-19`, `config.yaml:454`); the `competitor_sources: [web, g2, capterra, producthunt, appstore]` key is read by nothing. Name matching is `q in track or track in q` (`reviews.py:42`), which on this run matched **Going → "Going Balls"** (25 reviews: "Balls Game - I don't like this game it's wayyy to easy"), **Garbo → "Garbo Night Shift"**, and **Google Flights → "Google"** (25 reviews about Gemini). **52 of 722 stored reviews are of games or unrelated apps** and are printed in the report as incumbent gaps. Correctly matched reviews are about logins, bans and ads, not the mined pain, and the brief prompt accepts a wedge "grounded in a competitor's `weakness` or a real review quote" (`s10b_brief.py:47-50`) where `weakness` is itself LLM-authored in 9b, so a wedge can be an LLM citing an LLM and still pass the quote gate. Practitioners who mine reviews read the three- and four-star ones, because those buyers kept paying and describe what nearly stopped them, and warn that one-star reviews are both noisier and manufacturable ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/turn-g2-reviews-into-saas-ideas); [Source](https://evolveamz.com/review-mining-competitor-reviews-ecommerce/); [Source](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/nicolenguyen/Amazon-fake-review-problem)).

The report hides what the chain did measure. `s12_report.py:98-153` prints title, pitch, evidence and competitors, and no brief field at all (zero occurrences of brief, wedge, mvp or `has_review_evidence`); `ideas` has no provenance column, so a template fallback is indistinguishable from an LLM draft except by its hardcoded passport-bros titles ("TripFit Brief", "Streetwise Date Ledger"). The evidence line is `cluster["pains"][0]` whatever member carried the signal (`:124-128`), which is why rank 5 "lack of a database for digital nomads" is evidenced by "Same like Digital Nomad sounds better than broke dropshipper." And `reports/pbfamily0001.md` was written twelve minutes before commit `fbb07b3` rebuilt the scoring vocabulary, so the on-disk rank-1 score (55.11, demand 6.64) no longer matches the DB (67.40, demand 8.12). The runs themselves have `extract_provider`, `extract_model`, `prompt_version`, `code_version` and `config_hash` all NULL on the merged-run path (`webapp/app.py:935, 1222`), so neither production run is reproducible against a known prompt or model.

## What the meta measures, and what each stage measures instead

Across the practitioner canon, the 2024–2026 tool vendors and the requirements-engineering literature, the signals converge. The audience must already buy on value ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/30x500/is-30x500-right-for-me/)). The unit of evidence is recurrence across independent people, "what's showing up here a bunch on its own" ([Source](https://leanb2bbook.com/blog/alex-hillman-sales-safari/)), which on Reddit means distinct accounts, since 20% of users write 84% of comments ([Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02661)), and which every feedback vendor operationalises as distinct accounts plus the revenue behind them rather than mention counts ([Source](https://docs.dovetail.com/help/channels); [Source](https://www.enterpret.com/guides/how-to-compare-text-analytics-accuracy-across-cx-platforms-in-2026)). Evidence has a hierarchy: money already spent or help hired, then workarounds and tools tried, then a specific past incident with a cost, then emotion (a prompt to dig, not proof), and at the bottom hypotheticals and "I would pay" ([Source](https://theforge.mcmaster.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/The-Mom-Test.pdf); [Source](https://thebootstrappedfounder.com/the-shape-of-a-problem-in-the-wild/)). Frequency and severity are kept separate rather than collapsed, because "a rare but agonizing problem reads very differently from a common but mild annoyance" ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/docs/how-bigideasdb-works)), and depth beats breadth: "you get narrowness as a byproduct of optimizing for depth" ([Source](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html)). Gating conditions (budget, timing, buyer, solvability) multiply as ANDs; degrees of the same thing add ([Source](https://longform.asmartbear.com/problem/); [Source](https://strategyn.com/quantify-your-customers-unmet-needs)). Software fit is a checklist nobody publishes under one name but everyone uses: the pain recurs on a schedule, the person already has a workaround with a cost, that cost anchors to a business number ("The fully loaded cost of one employee is a good anchor" ([Source](https://kalzumeus.com/2013/05/23/selling-your-software-to-businesses-twiliocon-2012-presentation))), the outcome can be written as "minimize [metric] of [object]," and someone already spends on it. Negative checks sit beside it: prior failed attempts (tarpits), platform-only features, complainers who are not buyers, and communities whose recommended fixes are all free ("If every single recommendation is a free tool or resource, you might run into trouble charging for something comparable later" ([Source](https://thebootstrappedfounder.com/the-shape-of-a-problem-in-the-wild/))). Distinctiveness against a background corpus is measured with weighted log-odds under a Dirichlet prior ([Source](https://nlp-css-601-672.cs.jhu.edu/sp2024/files/0124.LogOdds.pdf); [Source](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/fightingwords.html)); emergence with a rate-over-baseline rule, with the caveat that burst detection tends "to surface signal consolidation rather than the early detection of weak signals" ([Source](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-srw.28.pdf); [Source](https://getthematic.com/insights/ai-powered-customer-feedback-theme-discovery)). Measurement discipline means a frozen, versioned gold set of 50–200 items including production failures, re-run on every prompt or model change ([Source](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/)), DBCV or run-to-run ARI for clustering ([Source](https://www.dbs.ifi.lmu.de/~zimek/publications/SDM2014/DBCV.pdf); [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17658)), and the knowledge that human coders themselves agree only 50–60% of the time on themes ([Source](https://getthematic.com/insights/ai-powered-customer-feedback-theme-discovery)). Finally, and by its own authors' statement, community text cannot prove willingness to pay: Sales Safari "is *not* customer interviews, not cold-calling, not surveys, or validation" ([Source](https://stackingthebricks.com/podcast/ep42-what-is-sales-safari-with-eteinne-garbugli/)); the documented cases show nine "interested" prospects converting to zero paying ([Source](https://www.andrewaskins.com/this-time-feels-different-my-updated-idea-validation-process/)) and thirty pre-payments as the bar that held ([Source](https://www.justinjackson.ca/why-its-not-enough-to-build-a-product-that-people-want)).

## Prioritized improvements

Ordered by leverage within each group. Each item gives the change, the file or config key, the evidence that motivates it, and the measurement that shows whether it worked.

### Larger redesign

13. **Add an audience gate before any text scoring.** Score each corpus on Walling's B2C / B2A / B2B / B2E axis ([Source](https://www.startupsfortherestofus.com/wp-content/uploads/The-5-PM-Idea-Evaluation-Framework.pdf)), the share of recommended tools that are paid versus free (Kahl), and the share of posters with buyer-role markers ("I run", "we pay", "our clients") versus consumer-role markers; refuse to rank a corpus that scores consumer and free. Point collection at professional watering holes (trade forums, vertical subreddits, support forums) where YC's current requests and Cohen's good-market list concentrate: calendar-driven paperwork, compliance, invoicing, permits ([Source](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs); [Source](https://mtlynch.io/notes/designing-the-ideal-bootstrapped-business/)). Evidence: every run is consumer lifestyle; 0 of 14 software-shaped on `2a926f2fad56`; Reddit F1 0.599 as the worst source; "10x less noise" in niche professional subs. Measure: on one B2B corpus, product-shaped themes in the top 25 by hand count (5 of 15 today, target ≥ 15 of 25), and the WTP floor share.

14. **Replace keyword demand with evidence-field extraction and a rubric judge.** Extend 3a to emit `money_spent` (amount, currency, period), `tools_mentioned`, `alternatives_sought`, `is_question`, `role_markers`, and `tense` alongside `workaround`; compute demand components as shares of distinct authors carrying each evidence class (BigIdeasDB's four-part signal: frequency, intensity 4+/5, quantified cost, weak existing solutions ([Source](https://bigideasdb.com/docs/pain-point-analysis))); make 7b a binary judge per criterion with order-swapped repeats and expected-value scoring ([Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21117)), validated against ~100 labelled spans per criterion with TPR and TNR reported ([Source](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/)); keep frequency and severity as separate columns in the report rather than one number. Evidence: nothing in stage 6 is LLM-judged; workaround at 2% and unused; the vendor builders who shipped something all converged on explicit pain / demand / WTP scores. Measure: per-criterion gold set with kappa > 0.6 against a human; top-25 product-shaped share; number of top-25 themes carrying a named price or paid tool.

15. **Dedupe at the claim level and normalise against a background.** Merge themes by label-embedding similarity before rank and count distinct authors across the merged theme; discount pains whose span duplicates another document's; compute per-theme weighted log-odds of its vocabulary against the other subreddits already in the DB (the `sources` table holds eight-plus communities) so generic internet grumbling scores low ([Source](https://nlp-css-601-672.cs.jhu.edu/sp2024/files/0124.LogOdds.pdf)); weight OP pains above reply pains with the new `thread_position` column. Evidence: 23 fragments of one pain; 309 duplicate spans; no background corpus; the independence critique that "five pages can repeat the same original complaint" ([Source](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/four-ways-mining-reddit-for-startup-ideas-went-wrong-for-me-and-one-disclosure-307e18f861)). Measure: fragments per hand-identified pain in the top 100 (23 → 1–2); distinctiveness z-scores reported per theme; "frustration with people"-type themes fall below the generic-chatter threshold.

16. **Add a tarpit lookup and the status-quo alternative.** For each top-50 theme, search Product Hunt, Crunchbase or the web for prior products and store count, survivors and years; treat "many attempts, no survivor, nothing changed" as a multiplier below `partial` ([Source](https://rosetta.to/u/ycombinator/tarpit-ideas-the-sequel)); record the status-quo alternative (spreadsheet, manual process, do nothing) as a first-class competitor in 9b ([Source](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/positioning-and-competition)). Evidence: the top-ranked ideas are dating-app products, the canonical consumer tarpit; "incumbent weaknesses" in briefs are generic ("Not region-focused"). Measure: share of top-10 ideas in YC tarpit categories (consumer discovery, coordination, dating) → 0; each brief names what the complainer does today.

17. **Close the loop with a behavioural rung and a ranking regression set.** Emit, per brief, an ebomb or landing-page test plan with thresholds fixed before launch (≥ 200 unique visitors; 5–15% email conversion from paid search; a pre-order count) ([Source](https://kromatic.com/real-startup-book/2-evaluative-market-experiments/value-proposition-test/landing-page-smoke-test/)), and record outcomes back on `ideas`; freeze a top-k list judged pairwise by one expert and track Kendall tau on every scoring change. Evidence: Sales Safari's authors disclaim validation; nine "interested" prospects → zero paid; thirty pre-payments as the bar that held; no benchmark exists for ranking as opposed to extraction. Measure: the only one that counts, pre-payments per idea; and tau ≥ 0.6 between the pipeline's top-k and the expert's on each release.

## Conclusion

The pipeline's failure is not an engineering failure but a measurement one, and it compounds in a specific order: a consumer audience that the method excludes before reading, an extractor built for recall whose precision pass was switched off, a clusterer tuned to maximise fragmentation, a demand formula in which the only money signal is a keyword count that is constant on two-thirds of themes, and a software gate that asks whether code *could* address a complaint rather than whether anyone *pays* to make it go away. Each stage was tuned honestly against the stage before it, which is why the arithmetic reproduces and the pathology survives: a well-measured proxy is still a proxy. The literature's answer is consistent across Hoy, Fitzpatrick, Cohen, YC and the vendors who publish benchmarks: evidence of spend and workaround outranks evidence of emotion, gates multiply and degrees add, and no amount of text proves willingness to pay.

The practical implication is that the cheap fixes (items 1–6) will raise the product-shaped share of the top 25 from roughly one in three toward one in two on the existing corpus, but the ceiling on a passport-bros corpus is low whatever the formula. The redesign that changes the game is items 13 and 14 together: choose a community whose members already buy software, then measure what they already pay for and work around, and treat the ranked list as the input to a pre-sale, not as the answer.
